DOWA CHESS CLUB TO HOST DOWA BOMA CHESS TOURNAMENT ON SEPTEMBER 5

Dowa, Malawi — September 4, 2026

Chess enthusiasts in Dowa are set for an exciting day of competition as Dowa Chess Club prepares to host the Dowa Boma Chess Tournament on September 5, 2026.

The tournament is expected to bring together chess players from Dowa and surrounding areas, providing an opportunity for players to test their skills and compete for top honours.

The event will also provide an important platform for promoting chess in the district and encouraging more young people to take up the game.

Chess lovers and supporters are encouraged to come and witness the competition as players battle it out on the board.

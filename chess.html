DOWA CHESS CLUB TO HOST DOWA BOMA CHESS TOURNAMENT ON SEPTEMBER 5

Dowa, Malawi — September 4, 2026

Chess enthusiasts from across the Central Region are set to converge at Nanthomba Secondary School on Saturday, September 5, for the Dowa Boma Chess Tournament, organized by Dowa Chess Club in conjunction with the Central Region Chess League (CRCL) and Central Region Chess League Zone 2.1.

The tournament, which is being promoted under the slogan “Checkmate Your Limits!”, is expected to attract players competing across nine rounds.

According to Marcus Kalima, games will start at 10:00 AM, with each player allocated 15 minutes per game.

The tournament also carries the Nchotsa Matsile of Malawi 2026 Open Tournament branding.

The event has received support from several sponsors, including CRCL Chairperson, CRCL Zone 2.1 Coordinator, CRCL Zone 2.1 Committee, and WFM Linda Jambo, according to Marcus Kalima, one of the organisers of the tournament.

The tournament is expected to provide an important platform for chess players in Dowa and the wider Central Region to test their skills, gain competitive experience and strengthen the local chess community.

With the Central Region Chess League involved, the event also highlights the growing efforts to promote organized chess competitions and develop the sport at grassroots level.
